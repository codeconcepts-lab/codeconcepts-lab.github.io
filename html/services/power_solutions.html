End-to-End Power Management

At CEMEX Project Power Solutions, we believe power should be reliable, efficient, and always within reach. We design, develop, and operate tailored power generation and distribution facilities, delivering consistent, scalable, and high-quality energy solutions for industries, businesses, and communities.

Our fully managed Independent Power Producer (IPP) model guarantees uninterrupted, cost-efficient energy with zero capital outlay. We handle everything—from asset provision and skilled operations to comprehensive maintenance and fuel management—allowing you to focus on your core business while we ensure a dependable power supply.

Our Core Offerings

We provide a comprehensive suite of power solutions designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

Renewable Energy Solutions

We design, build, and operate solar, gas, and diesel power plants to produce clean, reliable energy for the grid or specific clients.

Project Development

Our team expertly manages all permits, licenses, and infrastructure development required for successful renewable energy initiatives.

Independent Power Producer (IPP)

We operate gas, diesel, solar, and hybrid plants to deliver uninterrupted electricity for malls, estates, industries, and other large-scale clients.

Net Metering

We provide on-site system installation, allowing customers to be billed for their net energy usage and optimize costs.
