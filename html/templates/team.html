Power Provision

At CEMEX Project Power Solutions, we believe power should be reliable, efficient, and always within reach. We design, develop, and operate tailored power generation and distribution facilities, delivering consistent, scalable, and high-quality energy solutions for industries, businesses, and communities. Our goal is to ensure uninterrupted power supply, empowering our clients to operate with confidence and drive continuous growth.

Energy Solutions

With deep expertise in delivering scalable and reliable energy systems, we go beyond strategy to provide fully managed solutions tailored to client needs. Our Power Services are built on reliability, efficiency, and continuity-backed by practical, end-to-end execution.

We deliver Independent Power Producer (IPP) solutions that guarantee uninterrupted, cost-efficient energy. Our services include asset provision (generators and solar), skilled operations, and comprehensive maintenance. Through usage-based billing, zero capital outlay, and fuel-secure systems that eliminate losses, we take full responsibility for power management-allowing clients to focus on their core business while we ensure dependable energy supply.

Core Offerings of Power Services

  1. Renewable Energy Solutions: Designing, building, and operating Solar, Gas, Diesel and other power plants to produce clean energy for the grid or specific clients.
  2. Project Development: Managing permits, licenses, and infrastructure development for renewable energy initiatives.
  3. Independent Power Producer (IPP): Operating Gas, Diesel, Solar, and Hybrid plants to deliver uninterrupted electricity for malls, estates, industries, and large-scale clients.
  4. Net Metering: On-site installation of systems where customers are billed for net energy usage.
