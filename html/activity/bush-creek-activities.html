Activities at Bush Creek

Relax and Explore on the Farm

Enjoy the peace and natural beauty right here at Bush Creek. Take a walk, relax by the water, or simply soak in the tranquil farm atmosphere.

Enjoy the following activities that can be found at our Bush Creek Dam

  • Swimming: Take a refreshing dip in our dam.
  • Kayaking: Paddle around the dam and enjoy the scenery.
  • Windsurfing: Catch the wind and glide across the water.
  • Fishing: Try your luck at catching bass in our dam. Please bring your own fishing rods. Catch-and-release policy.
  • River: Enjoy our beautiful river
  • Other Activities: Enjoy our beautiful River and Farm Walks.

    • Important Notice: All activities are at your own risk.

Other Facilities at the Dam:

  • Braai Area: Enjoy a barbecue with family and friends.
  • Sunbathing: Soak up the sun and enjoy the scenic views.
  • Jetty Jumping: Take the leap off our jetty into the dam for an adrenaline rush!

Viewing and Riding Horses

  • Admire our magnificent horses from a safe distance. Please note that interacting with the horses is at your own risk.
  • If you're an experienced rider and would like to ride our horses, please contact the farmer in advance to arrange a ride. Please note that riding is only permitted with the farmer's supervision and approval. You may enquire about Riding Fees with your host.
